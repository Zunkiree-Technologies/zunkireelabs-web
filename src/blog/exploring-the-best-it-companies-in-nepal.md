## Overview of Nepal's IT Landscape

Nepal's information technology (IT) sector has witnessed remarkable growth over the last decade. This vibrant industry is characterized by a blend of emerging startups and well-established companies that have made significant contributions to the region's economy and technology landscape. With the increase in internet penetration and mobile connectivity, Nepal has seen a surge in demand for digital solutions, both among businesses looking to optimize their operations and consumers seeking enhanced services. Numerous IT companies have emerged, focusing on innovative technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, and mobile application development. This boom is not just limited to urban centers like Kathmandu; it is rapidly spreading to other regions, enabling a diverse array of companies to contribute to the national IT landscape. As a result, the Nepali IT industry is not only serving local markets but is also garnering attention on a global scale, positioning itself as an attractive destination for tech investment and talent. The government's recognition of the sector's potential has further catalyzed growth, with various initiatives aimed at fostering a friendly environment for IT startups and professionals.
