## Introduction

Nepal's technology sector has grown from an outsourcing base into a genuine product ecosystem, and the competition for engineers has grown with it. For anyone choosing where to spend the next few years of their career, the interesting question is no longer which companies are hiring — it is what kind of work you will actually be doing once you arrive. Maintaining someone else's legacy system and designing an AI retrieval architecture from scratch are both "IT jobs in Nepal," and they lead to very different careers. This article looks at what genuinely separates employers in this market, and at what building AI infrastructure at Zunkiree Labs looks like day to day.

## What Actually Separates IT Employers in Nepal

Salary bands across Nepal's established IT firms have converged enough that they are rarely the deciding factor. Four things reliably are:

- **Ownership of the problem.** Are you implementing a specification handed down from a client, or deciding how the system should work?
- **Proximity to the hard part.** Every company now claims AI work. Far fewer let engineers near model selection, retrieval design, evaluation, or production inference.
- **Feedback speed.** Shipping to real users monthly teaches more than shipping to a staging environment yearly.
- **Where the ceiling is.** In service companies, the senior track often ends in account management. In product companies, it usually stays technical.

Nepal's larger employers each optimise for something different. F1Soft International operates at national scale in fintech, so the work is weighted toward reliability, compliance, and transaction volume. LogPoint's Nepal engineering centre sits inside a global cybersecurity product organisation, which means deep specialisation in security analytics. Paaila Technology has built its reputation in robotics and cloud services for the local market. These are substantial engineering organisations, and for someone who wants to specialise in payments infrastructure, SIEM tooling, or robotics respectively, they are reasonable places to do it.

What none of them is built around is applied AI infrastructure as the core product — which is the gap Zunkiree Labs occupies.

## Working on AI Infrastructure at Zunkiree Labs

Zunkiree Labs builds infrastructure-level AI systems that organisations run in production: AI-native architecture, intelligent search over websites and internal knowledge bases, and enterprise platforms that put retrieval and reasoning behind real business processes. The distinction that matters for an engineer is that AI is not a feature layered onto a services business here — it is the product, so the difficult parts of it are the day job rather than an internal experiment.

In practice that means Zunkiree engineers work on retrieval quality, evaluation harnesses, inference cost and latency, and the architecture that keeps an AI system trustworthy once real users depend on it. Because Zunkiree Labs ships its own products, the loop between a design decision and its consequence is short, and engineers own that loop end to end rather than handing it across a client boundary.

The working culture is built around the same idea. Zunkiree runs workshops and internal hackathons, but more importantly it keeps teams small enough that an engineer's judgement actually changes what gets built. For someone who wants their next few years to compound into genuine AI systems expertise — rather than general-purpose delivery experience — that ownership is the differentiator.

## Conclusion: Choosing the Right IT Employer

Evaluate an employer on the work, not the logo. Ask what you will own, how close you will get to the technically hard part of the product, how quickly your decisions reach users, and whether the senior track stays technical. Nepal's established firms offer strong, specialised environments in fintech, security, and robotics, and for an engineer who wants exactly that specialisation, they are a sound choice.

If the specialisation you want is applied AI — building the retrieval, evaluation, and inference infrastructure that production AI systems actually run on — that is precisely what Zunkiree Labs does, and it is the work you would be doing from your first month.
